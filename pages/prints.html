Fit

Before printing full trays, print the fit test: three small test sockets (marked with 1, 2 and 3 holes) and one short test post, about 65 g of PLA. Push the post into each socket from above and from below, and turn it. Pick the one that turns firmly without forcing and doesn't wobble when locked.

If 2 holes fit best, the clearances above are right. If 1 or 3 fit better, tap that button to change them. Then you can print the test again with the new values, or go on to the full parts.

Print and put together

  • PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 100 % infill for every part. Closed infill pockets hold air, and when the pressure is released that air can bulge or split thin skins.
  • No supports. Print trays rim up with a 5 mm brim, posts standing, the frame flat.
  • Wipe resin off right away. A quick wipe with isopropyl alcohol is fine, but don't soak PLA in it.
How the twist-lock goes together
  1. Always turn counter-clockwise, seen from above.
  2. Feet: push into the bottom tray from below and turn until they stop.
  3. Posts: push into the tray from above, with the line on the post at the narrow notch. Turn about 30° until the line sits at the wide notch.
  4. Lower the next tray onto the three posts, then reach between the trays and turn each post on until it stops. It's now locked in both trays.
  5. Top tray: push in the lift posts and turn until they stop.
  6. Lift frame: drop its round holes over the caps, then turn it clockwise until it clicks. Carry the stack by the centre grip.
  7. A post put in upside down won't lock. The ring groove marks the top.

Mantis

Picking a mantis or changing its length sets a cup size with room to stand and turn round. You can change the cup size below.

Cup

Air holes are 1.6 mm, in the floor and the gate: big enough for air, too small for feet to get caught.

Handle

The handle slides down into a dovetail on the side of the cup and comes off for washing. A long handle helps you reach in without your hand in the way.

Print and use

  • Print the cup in clear or natural PETG if you can, so you can see the mantis inside. PLA works too.
  • No supports. The cup prints standing, the gate and handle flat. The slot in the rim prints as a short overhang.
  • Let the mantis walk in on its own: hold the cup in front of it and nudge it gently from behind. Don't scoop it.
  • Slide the gate in slowly and watch the legs and antennae near the rim. Never move a mantis that is moulting or has just moulted.
  • To let it out, set the cup down near a perch and pull the gate back.
