Size

Width and depth are measured between the panel centres. The base holds the substrate, so the panels start above it and the front panel can slide up without spilling soil.

Width is measured across the flats, between panel centres. Each wall is a flat panel, clear acrylic or printed mesh, held at the corners by printed spines. The front wall is a sliding door.

The wall is either a clear PET sheet (0.5–1 mm, sold as "PET-Folie" or "PETG-Platte") rolled into grooves in the base and lid, or a printed mesh tube for full ventilation. You open it by lifting the lid, or through a feeding hatch.

Floor pegs stand on the base under the substrate, 40 mm apart. Pots, dishes and twig holders made with a floor socket push onto them, so they stay put when the mantis climbs on them.

Walls and door

The swing door turns on a rod printed along its edge, which snaps into three printed clips on the frame. No pin, screws or glue. It closes onto a raised rim, held by two round magnets or by a printed rib that clicks into a groove in the door.

Swappable tile panels are a frame with square openings. Each opening takes a printed mesh tile or a clear acrylic tile, held by small printed turn buttons, so you can change which parts of the wall are clear or ventilated at any time.

Clip rails are small channels on the inside of printed mesh panels, 40 mm apart. Accessories made with a clip back drop into them and click in place, like IKEA Skådis.

The lid is always a printed mesh, so the mantis has a grippy ceiling to hang from when it moults. Clear panels are acrylic sheet cut to the sizes on the right. Mesh sides add cross-ventilation, which mantises from warm, humid places need.

Lid and lights

The hatch sits where you put it. If something is in the way, such as a light window, it moves to the nearest free spot. A magnet hatch lifts off; a plug hatch pushes into the opening; a twist-lock hatch pushes in and locks with a short turn, so a mantis can't push it open. Plug and twist-lock hatches need no magnets.

Printer and filament

Set to a Creality K2 Plus (350 × 350 × 350 mm). Change it if you print on another machine.

Before you print

  • Print the base and lid flat, the posts lying down, and mesh panels flat. Each file is already turned that way.
  • Glue the posts into the base only after a dry fit, so you can still slide the panels in.
  • If you feed fruit flies to small nymphs, they can get through printed mesh. Glue fine fabric mesh under the lid and mesh panels for that stage.
  • Wash printed parts and let them air out for a day before the mantis moves in.
