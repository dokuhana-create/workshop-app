Animal

Size

Width and depth are measured between the panel centres. The base holds the substrate, so the panels start above it and the front panel can slide up without spilling soil.

Panels

The swing door hangs on a hinge along its left edge, with a 1.75 mm filament strand as the pin, and closes onto a raised rim with two round magnets. It opens outwards, so the mantis isn't disturbed from above.

The lid is always a printed mesh, so the mantis has a grippy ceiling to hang from when it moults. Clear panels are acrylic sheet cut to the sizes on the right. Mesh sides add cross-ventilation, which mantises from warm, humid places need.

Printer and filament

Set to a Creality K2 Plus (350 × 350 × 350 mm). Change it if you print on another machine.

Before you print

  • Print the base and lid flat, the posts lying down, and mesh panels flat. Each file is already turned that way.
  • Glue the posts into the base only after a dry fit, so you can still slide the panels in.
  • If you feed fruit flies to small nymphs, they can get through printed mesh. Glue fine fabric mesh under the lid and mesh panels for that stage.
  • Wash printed parts and let them air out for a day before the mantis moves in.

Mounting

Magnet mounting holds the piece against a panel with a second magnet on the outside, so nothing needs drilling. Disc magnets of 10 × 3 mm hold small pieces through 3 mm acrylic. Glue each magnet into its pocket, and check the poles face the right way before the glue sets.
