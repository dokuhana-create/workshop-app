About these forms

  • Brush strokes uses the Ma Shan Zheng font and Print uses Noto Serif SC Black, both under the SIL Open Font License.
  • In the two seal styles, 李 is built from 木 over 子, and 岩 from 山 over 石, drawn in two styles: flowing curves nearer to small seal script, or the even, squared line of Han seals. They are drawn for this tool, not copied from a historical seal.
  • Have someone who reads seal script check them before you carve them in stone.
  • The stamp itself is mirrored, so the impression reads correctly.

Before you pour

  • Test on a small area first. Dragon Skin and Sorta-Clear are platinum silicones, and they can stay tacky against sulfur clay, latex, or freshly cast epoxy, polyester, or urethane.
  • Be especially careful with resin-printed masters, and wash and cure them fully. A clear acrylic lacquer usually prevents curing problems.
  • Sand or seal a PLA master, because silicone copies every layer line.
  • Seal the seam around a frame and base from the outside so the silicone can't leak out.
  • Use a release agent before casting epoxy into the finished mould.

Mix ratios, cure times, and curing advice come from Smooth-On's technical data charts and Dragon Skin bulletin. Confirm them on your own product's label.
